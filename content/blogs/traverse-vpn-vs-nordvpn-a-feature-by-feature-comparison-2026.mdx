### **Protocol Deep Dive: WireGuard vs OpenVPN vs IKEv2**

Understanding which protocol to use and when is one of the most practical things a VPN user can learn. Traverse VPN gives you all three options. Here is when each one gives you the best result.

**Use WireGuard when:** You want the fastest possible connection for everyday browsing, streaming, or gaming. WireGuard's lean codebase means lower latency and higher throughput compared to OpenVPN. It is the right default for most situations.

**Use OpenVPN UDP when:** You need a well-tested, widely compatible protocol on a standard open network. UDP mode is faster than TCP and works well on home networks and most mobile connections.

**Use OpenVPN TCP when:** You are on a restrictive network — a hotel, office, school, or airport — that blocks or throttles VPN traffic. TCP mode is slower but much harder to detect and block.

**Use IKEv2 when:** You are on a mobile device that regularly switches between Wi-Fi and cellular data. IKEv2 handles network transitions almost invisibly, maintaining your VPN connection without requiring you to reconnect manually.

This level of protocol flexibility is not standard across the VPN market. Many providers offer only one or two protocols, forcing users onto a single option regardless of their situation.
